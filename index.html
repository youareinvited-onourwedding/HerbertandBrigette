Our Story

We first met in the quiet, ordinary days of high school, where everything felt simple and new. Between shared laughter, stolen glances, and the kind of conversations that only young hearts understand, our story began. It wasn’t perfect, but it was real—our first experience of love, innocent and full of promise. For a while, we believed that what we had would last forever. But life, in its unpredictable way, led us down different paths. We were young, still learning who we were, and before we knew it, our love slipped through our fingers. We said goodbye, not fully understanding that we were also leaving behind a piece of ourselves in each other.

Years passed, and life shaped us in ways we never imagined. We met new people, chased dreams, faced heartbreaks, and grew stronger on our own. Yet somehow, through all the changes, there was always a quiet memory of what we once had—a love that never truly disappeared, only resting in the background of our lives. Then, when we least expected it, fate stepped in and brought us back together. In that moment, it felt as though time folded in on itself. The familiarity was still there, but now it was deeper, wiser, and more certain.

We found ourselves rediscovering each other, not as the teenagers we once were, but as the people we had become. The spark we thought we had lost came back to life, stronger than ever, as if it had been waiting for the right time to shine again. This time, our love was no longer just about fleeting moments—it was about choosing each other, every single day, with understanding, patience, and a kind of devotion that only comes from having once lost and found again.

Now, as we stand on the edge of forever, preparing to become husband and wife, our story feels like something beautifully written by fate itself. Every goodbye, every year apart, every lesson learned led us back to this moment. And this time, we know—we are no longer just two people who fell in love, but two souls who were meant to find their way back to each other, and to stay, for always.
